The problem

Painters have the hardest job online

A photograph of a painting loses its scale, its texture and its true color. So an artist's site has to work twice as hard as anyone else's — and most of them are not even doing the easy part.

Nobody can buy anything

Prices on the page, no checkout anywhere. The visitor has to email and wait, and most of them simply do not.

The town is never named

Google cannot show an artist to somebody searching "art near me" if the website never says where the studio is. This is the most common miss we find, by far.

The pictures are invisible

Search engines cannot see a painting — they read the description attached to it. On most artist sites, most images have none.

Prints are left on the table

An original sells once. A print sells the same painting for years, and cards sell it every week — but only if there is somewhere to buy them.
