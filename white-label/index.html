Straight Answers

What you get — and what we won't do

We'd rather tell you the limits up front than surprise you later. That honesty is the whole reason people work with us twice.

✅ What's included

  • The full source — you own your copy of the build, not a rented login.
  • Brand tokens in one place — colors, fonts, logo, and contact info at the top of the file.
  • Mobile-first and fast — every product is checked on a phone before it ships.
  • SEO groundwork — titles, descriptions, schema, sitemap, and a share image.
  • Setup notes in plain English — written for a business owner, not an engineer.
  • No badge, no backlink, no credit — it's your product once it leaves here.
  • Optional ongoing support — text a real person in New Bern, not a ticket queue.

⚠️ What we won't white-label

  • Another client's work. The pattern we designed is ours to license. A client's brand, photos, and copy are theirs — we never resell them.
  • Regulated products. No investing, lending, medical, or health-claim tools. Those need licenses and lawyers, not templates.
  • Anything requiring a license we don't hold. If it needs a professional to sign off, we'll say so and stop.
  • Claims we can't stand behind. No fake reviews, no invented numbers, no guaranteed rankings. Ever.
